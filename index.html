Team Setup Guide: How to Connect

1

Install WireGuard

Download the official free WireGuard app for your operating system:

Windows macOS Android iOS
2

Select Your Name & Desired Country

Choose your name from the top header (Alex, Maya, Liam, Elena, or Jordan). Then click on Germany, France, or Australia.

3

Import and Activate

On PC/Mac: Click "Download .conf" and drag the file directly into WireGuard. Click Activate.

On Phone: Open WireGuard, tap + ➔ "Create from QR code", and scan the QR code on this screen.

4

Verify Your Location

Visit ifconfig.me or whatismyipaddress.com. You will see that your IP location is now in Frankfurt, Paris, or Sydney!
